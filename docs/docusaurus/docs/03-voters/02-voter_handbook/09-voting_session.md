## Your session

Your session starts when you log in. It ends when you log out, or
automatically when you have been inactive for too long.

## When the session ends by itself

The voting portal and the ballot verifier end your session after 15 minutes
without any interaction from you. The voting system then also ends your login.
This protects your account if you leave your device.

- After that you must log in again.
- A ballot you had not cast is not cast. Open it again with "Click to Vote" and
  fill it in again.
- A vote you had already cast stays cast.

Your election may warn you before your session ends, with the message "Your
session is going to expire." and the time you have left to cast your vote.
Finish or cast your ballot before the time runs out.

## Log out

1. At the top of the page, select your name (it follows "Welcome,") to open the
   menu.
2. Select "Logout".
3. The voting portal asks "Are you sure you want to logout?" and says "You are
   about to close this application. This action can not be undone."
4. Select "OK" to log out. Select "Close" to stay logged in.

Your session ends at once.

On the confirmation screen, "Finish" also logs you out when you have no more
ballots to vote on.

## When you have finished

:::warning

**Do not leave your device while you are logged in.** Before you leave it, log
out.

:::

1. Log out.
2. Close all browser tabs and windows of the voting portal and the ballot
   verifier, and then the browser.
3. If you audited a ballot, delete the file you downloaded for the audit. It
   shows the selections of the audited ballot.
4. On a device that other people also use, also clear the browser history and
   the downloads list.
