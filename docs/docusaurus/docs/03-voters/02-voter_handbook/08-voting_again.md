## Whether you may vote again

Your election organizer decides, under the electoral regulation, whether you
may cast more than one vote in a ballot, and how many. Unless your election
organizer tells you otherwise, you can vote only once: after you have cast your
vote, the ballot shows "Voted" and you cannot select "Click to Vote" for it
again.

## How to vote again

If your election allows it:

1. While voting is open, log in and open the "Ballot list". The ballot shows
   "Voted".
2. Select "Click to Vote" for the ballot.
3. Fill in, review and cast your ballot as described in
   [Casting Your Vote](05-casting_your_vote.md).
4. Keep the new Ballot ID. You can find each of your cast ballots with the
   ballot locator.

Only the vote you cast last is counted. It replaces your earlier vote in the
count.

## If you cannot vote again

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "You have exceeded the revote limit. Please contact support." | You have cast as many votes as your election allows. Your new vote was not cast. Your earlier vote stays valid. | Nothing, unless you think this is wrong. Then contact the election organizer. |
| "You have exceeded the allowed number of revotes. Please contact support" | The same as above. | The same as above. |
| "You have already voted in another area. Please contact support" | A vote of yours has already been cast in another area of the election. Your new vote was not cast. | Contact the election organizer. |

:::warning

**If the voting portal shows "Voted" although you have not cast a vote, someone
else may have used your login details.** Tell the election organizer at once.

:::
