The **Users and Roles** page holds the accounts of the administrators and trustees of the
tenant, and the roles that give them permissions. Voters are not on this page: they are on the
**Voters** tab of each election event.

## Open the page

In the menu on the left, click **Users and Roles**. The page has the header **Users and Roles**
and "General configuration".

The menu item needs the **View users and roles** permission. The page also needs **Read User**
or **Read Role**. Without them, the page shows "You don't have permission to access users or
roles.".

## Tabs

| Tab | Use | Permission | Reference page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Users** | The accounts of the administrators and trustees. | **Read User** | [Users](Users/admin_portal_reference_user-manual_users-and-roles_users.md) |
| **Roles** | The roles and their permissions. | **Read Role** | [Roles](Roles/admin_portal_reference_user-manual_users-and-roles_roles.md) |

The list of all permissions is in [Permissions](Permissions/admin_portal_reference_user-manual_users-and-roles_permissions.md).
