The **TEMPLATES** tab is in **Settings**. It is **not in use**. You do not have to set it.

## How to open it

1. In the menu on the left, click **Settings**.
2. Click the **TEMPLATES** tab.

## What the tab shows

| Item | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Mails** | A switch. It is disabled. You cannot change it. |
| **SMS** | A switch. It is disabled. You cannot change it. |

## What to use instead

To manage the messages and documents that the system sends or makes, use **Templates** in the
menu on the left. See [Templates](../templates/admin_portal_reference_user-manual_templates.md).
