braid v0.6 — mixnet emulator

interactive, browser-hosted trustees against a live b4

Manages one Setup (a committee: manager + trustee keys + configuration) and drives the protocol one round at a time, with each browser trustee talking to a running b4 over HTTP+S3 and persisting to its own IndexedDB store. The DKG runs once; each tally then runs over its own child board unioned with the DKG (§8.2). Requires localstack.ps1 + b4.ps1 running.
